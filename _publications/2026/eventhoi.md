abstract: >-
  We present EventHOI, an event-driven hand-object interaction (HOI) detection and compression system for smartglasses that uses low-power event sensing as an always-on sentinel to trigger RGB capture only when HOIs likely occur, with IMU-based egomotion cancellation to suppress head-induced events and residual-event saliency to guide HOI-aware compression for VLM-based HOI logging. On a smartglasses prototype with 10 hours of real-world data and a 50-hour egocentric dataset, EventHOI reduces always-on backend power by 5.8--10.4x, uplink bandwidth by 3.0x, and RGB capture by 1.5--3.8x compared to RGB-only baselines, with minimal HOI recognition accuracy loss.
